Three
Things.

Make a few decisions once. Keep them close.

A small Mac desktop app for the priorities that deserve to stay in view. No inbox, streaks, scores, or project system.

  1. TodayWhat needs your attention now.
  2. This weekThe work that should not get lost in the days.
  3. This monthThe bigger thing worth protecting.

Small on purpose.

Choose your three. Keep them visible in a slim Mini bar. Cross them off when they are real. Export a plain-text copy when you want one.

Three Things stores priorities locally on your Mac. View the project on GitHub.
