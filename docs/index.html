Three Things is a small Mac app for today, this week, and this month. No inbox, projects, or streaks to maintain.

Why I made it.

I wanted a small place to keep the few things I actually meant to do. Not another system to organize, and not a longer list to feel behind on.

So I made Three Things. Choose a few priorities. Keep them close. Give one your attention. When they are done, let that count.

— Bud

Small on purpose.

  • Three things at a time.Separate priorities for today, this week, and this month. Use List to see all three, Focus for one, or Compact for a slim bar beside your work.
  • A little room to plan.Arrows beside the date let you review yesterday or write tomorrow’s list. One link brings you back to Today. No calendar to manage.
  • Space when you want it.Go full screen with the green Mac button or Control–Command–F. Choose a quiet photo, a plain color, or an illustrated meadow.
  • Done gets to count.Finish the three for a small “Well done.” Add optional tasks if you want to keep going. Clear today has Undo, so a fresh start need not be permanent.
  • Saved on your Mac.No account or cloud sync. Your priorities save locally, and backgrounds work offline. Export a plain-text copy whenever you want.

Open it on your Mac.

This download is for Apple Silicon Macs, not Intel Macs. It is an early, ad-hoc-signed build. Apple has not notarized it.

  1. Download the ZIP and double-click it to extract Three Things.app.
  2. Move the app to Applications, then open it.
  3. If macOS blocks it because the developer cannot be verified, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security. Find the Three Things notice and choose Open Anyway, then confirm Open. Only proceed if you trust this download and its source.

Do not disable system-wide protections. If macOS reports malware or says the app will damage your computer, do not bypass that warning. Read Apple’s guidance.

Updates are manual. Quit the app before replacing it. Local saving is not encryption or a backup; see the privacy and backup notes.
